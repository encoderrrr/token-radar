How the value is worked out

implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity

The multiple is the category's own going rate: the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the token-trading protocols in that category, shrunk halfway toward the global median so a thin category cannot swing the value; a category with fewer than three trading comps takes the global median. Growth is the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%…+100% and halved. Maturity runs from 0.6 for a new protocol to 1.0 for one listed at least three years ago; a protocol with no listing date is counted at 365 days. No name is special-cased: the same formula scores every row, and the table below is the one the snapshot produced.
